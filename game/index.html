RIDGE RUNNER. Use 1, 2, or 3 to choose a mission, Enter to fly. W/S throttle, A/D pitch, Space boost, P pause, R restart, Escape mission menu. V switches between side and chase views and restarts the mission. Chase: A/D bank, ArrowUp/ArrowDown climb and descend, W/S throttle, Space boost. Drag the throttle on the right of the instrument panel.
